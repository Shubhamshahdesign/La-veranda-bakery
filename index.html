A French bakery on Hoxton Street. Croissants, cakes and breakfast from 6am.

La Véranda is a small French-style bakery and café near Hoxton Street Market. The counter is full from first thing: butter croissants, fruit tarts, lemon meringue, opera cake and brownies. Behind it, a red espresso machine and a kitchen that does a full English, sweet and savoury crêpes, quiche and warm ciabatta.

Sit in, take away, or grab a coffee on the way to work. Prices stay sensible and the music stays low, so you can actually talk.

Plenty of tables, and quiet enough to talk

Inside it's whitewashed wood, trailing plants and fairy lights. Long tables run along the front windows, and there are a few seats outside on Hoxton Street when the weather is on your side.

Good for a slow breakfast, a working coffee or a quick bite on your own. Kids welcome, and the entrance is step free.
